## Contraction Rules (Critical for Sounding Human)
 
### Always Use Contractions:
 
**Personal pronouns:**
- I'm (not I am)
- I'll (not I will)
- I've (not I have)
- I'd (not I would)
- You're (not you are)
- You'll (not you will)
**Negatives:**
- Don't (not do not)
- Can't (not cannot)
- Won't (not will not)
- Isn't (not is not)
- Hasn't (not has not)
- Doesn't (not does not)
**Common phrases:**
- That's (not that is)
- Here's (not here is)
- There's (not there is)
- It's (not it is)
- What's (not what is)
**Why this matters**: AI rarely uses contractions naturally. Humans use them constantly in informal communication.
 
### Exception:
Only write out full forms for emphasis: "I will NOT use generic templates" (not "I won't" here).
